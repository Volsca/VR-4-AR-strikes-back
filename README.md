# VR 4 AR strikes back
 Internship at INRIA main project :

 A study aimed to évaluate if the presence of a virtual avatar is a viable form of assistance towards interacting in video mixed reality. 
 Associated with a IEEE research paper : A. Cheymol, J. Wallace, J. Yoneyama, R. Fribourg, J. -M. Normand and F. Argelaguet, "Measuring the Impact of Objects' Physicalization, Avatar Appearance, and their Consistency on Pick-and-Place Performance in Augmented Reality," in IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, vol. 31, no. 5, pp. 2268-2276, May 2025, doi: 10.1109/TVCG.2025.3549151.
